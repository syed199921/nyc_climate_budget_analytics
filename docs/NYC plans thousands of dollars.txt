NYC plans thousands of dollars in capital spending across budget lines and thousands of projects, yet its data on climate alignment and investment lives in a single file. Decision-makers find it difficult to see how much of the spending in each asset category is climate-aligned, which budget lines are significantly non-aligned for emissions, how much funding falls in flood- and heat-vulnerable areas, how many projects are still awaiting a climate rating, and how much planned funding do they account for, and how  plans changed from FY2025, FY2026, and FY2027.
