Part 1: Business Understanding

Important Notes:

-  Climate Budgeting is a novel and 
transformative decision-making process that we have embedded within the budget process to 
evaluate the impact of the city’s investments on its ambitious climate goals.


- This initiative provides data that guide us towards investments that will improve the 
health and quality of life of New Yorkers by reducing emissions, enhancing air quality, and 
increasing heat and flood resiliency while, at the same time, elevating environmental justice 
for communities of color.
